About the Expo

Organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF), the Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 brings together smallholder farmers, processors, roasters, and global buyers to celebrate Uganda's world-renowned Robusta and Arabica coffee heritage.

Over three dynamic days on the shores of Lake Victoria, participants will explore sustainable farming innovations, value-addition technologies, climate-resilient agronomy, and direct international trade partnerships.
