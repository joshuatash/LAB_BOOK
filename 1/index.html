Welcome to the IT Department

The Department of Information Technology at ISBAT University is committed to delivering excellence in modern computing, software engineering, and network systems. We equip our students with practical, future-ready skills required to thrive in today's fast-paced digital economy.

Through state-of-the-art computer laboratories, expert mentorship, and robust project-based learning, our programs foster creativity, critical problem-solving, and technical innovation.

Programmes Offered

  • Bachelor of Science in Applied Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science & Software Engineering
  • Diploma in Information Technology
  • Postgraduate Diploma in Information Security & Systems Management
